# scroll-art
For my ASCII art project, I created an ice cream cone, because it is my favorite dessert, and a pumpkin because Halloween is coming up soon.


# process and citations
Both the ice cream and jack-o-lantern came from my imagination. Before starting, I sketched out my ideas on graph paper. After I had my plan, I input the individual characters into their 2d locations.

I also created a way in which my art won't overlap in terms of length and width
using isBlank, and only generating a new piece of art 12 spaces to the right
of the previous one.
